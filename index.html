Rules of thumb at the table
Open to $3, plus $1 for every limper already in. Same size with every hand you open.
The number above is your ceiling, not a target. If the betting goes past it, fold, even if you already put money in.
Small pairs and suited connectors want a cheap, crowded pot. If it costs more than ~$6 and you are not getting 3+ callers, fold them.
Offsuit trash (K7o, Q8o, J6o, anything 9-high) is a fold no matter how cheap. That is where money leaks.
AKo / AQs are strong but they are drawing hands. They flop a pair 1 time in 3.
Multiway kills big offsuit cards and helps suited/connected ones. The tool already shifts for this.
Numbers worth memorising
AA vs one random hand: 85% · AA vs 8 random hands: ~31%
Pair vs two overcards (99 vs AK): ~55/45, basically a coinflip
AK vs QQ: 43% · AK vs AQ: 74% · AK unpaired misses the flop 67% of the time
Any pocket pair flops a set: ~12% (1 in 8.5) — so set-mining needs ~15x the call behind
Two suited cards make a flush by the river: 6.5%
Odds you are dealt AA: 1 in 221 · any pair: 1 in 17
